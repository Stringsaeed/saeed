
These are the most important skills I want in an AI-assisted engineering workflow.

## Verification

Make sure the changes actually work.

Useful skills:

- [Create Verification Skill](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack/skills/create-verification-skill/SKILL.md)
- [Maintain Verification Skill](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/main/pstack/skills/maintain-verification-skill/SKILL.md)
- [Principle: Prove It Works](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/main/pstack/skills/principle-prove-it-works/SKILL.md)

## Remove AI slop

Remove stereotypical AI writing, replace vague language with concrete language, and let the author's actual personality show through.

Useful tools:

- [Unslop](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack/skills/unslop/SKILL.md)
- [Humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer)
- [anti-slop](https://github.com/dmmulroy/anti-slop), an Oxlint plugin

## Restraint

Do not overengineer the solution. Remove unnecessary complexity from the code and design.

- [Principle: Laziness Protocol](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack/skills/principle-laziness-protocol/SKILL.md)
