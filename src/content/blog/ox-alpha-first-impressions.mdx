
Ox Alpha feels too good to be true.

If you have not heard about it yet, it is a stealth model that was almost completely free through OpenCode and several model gateways when I tried it.

Benchmarks aside, it showed surprising compute power. To me, it felt like a free version of Grok 4.6.

At the time of writing, it was also available free for the week through OpenCode Go and Zen. If it is still available when you read this, it is worth trying.
